How to use the board

Send a few saved designs, not a perfect plan

You do not need to know the exact final design before messaging. Send two or three Pinterest saves and mention what you like: the flowers, the spacing, the finger details, the Arabic flow, or the overall mood. Tanya can then guide the final mehndi design so it suits your hand and occasion.
