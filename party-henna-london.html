Quick answers for event bookings.

How many guests can have party henna?

The number of guests depends on the event time, design size and level of detail. Send your guest count and time window so I can suggest realistic design options.

What designs work best for party henna?

Party henna usually works best with neat mini designs, floral trails, Arabic-inspired sections, finger details or simple guest designs that keep the event moving.
