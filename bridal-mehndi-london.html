Bridal FAQ

Quick answers before you enquire.

How early should I book bridal mehndi in London?

For bridal mehndi, it is best to enquire as early as possible with your wedding date, London area or venue, preferred timing and design inspiration so I can check availability and plan the right amount of design time.

What should I send before booking bridal mehndi?

Send your date, venue or postcode area, outfit or jewellery details, saved design ideas and how much coverage you want on hands, arms or feet.
